What is grass jelly?

Grass jelly (cincau, or chao kuay) is a dark, soft jelly made by boiling the leaves of a mint-family plant. It has a mild herbal taste, a smooth bite, and has been a street-side cooler across Southeast Asia for generations. We bottle it fresh with tea and fruit so you get the same drink in seconds.
